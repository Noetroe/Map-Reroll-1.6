[h1]Map Reroll (1.4/1.5/1.6)[/h1]

This update brings Map Reroll forward for RimWorld 1.6 while keeping support folders for 1.4 and 1.5.

Map Reroll lets you quickly randomize your starting map and steam geyser locations. Your starting scenario, colonists, items, and settlement setup stay the same.

The mod includes map previews, so instead of blindly rolling random maps, you can browse preview pages and choose the map you want.

[hr][/hr]

[h1]Features[/h1]

[list]
[*]Reroll the current starting map from an in-game preview grid.
[*]Reroll steam geyser locations.
[*]Keep your scenario, colonists, and starting items intact.
[*]Optional resource cost paid from unmined map resources.
[*]RimWorld 1.6 compatibility fixes.
[*]Optional integration with Map Preview for more accurate 1.6 previews.
[/list]

[hr][/hr]

[h1]Requirements[/h1]

[list]
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=818773962]HugsLib[/url] is required.
[*]Load HugsLib before Map Reroll.
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2800857642]Map Preview[/url] is optional, but recommended for the best preview accuracy on RimWorld 1.6.
[*]If using Map Preview, load it before Map Reroll.
[/list]

Suggested load order:

[code]
HugsLib
Map Preview (optional)
Map Reroll
[/code]

[hr][/hr]

[h1]Resource Cost[/h1]

Rerolling has a small price: unmined resources from the map.

This cost is optional and can be disabled in:

[code]
Options > Mod Settings
[/code]

[hr][/hr]

[h1]Warning[/h1]

Use your best judgment when rerolling and save your game first.

Map generation is one of RimWorld's more sensitive systems. This mod has been updated and tested around vanilla-style generation, but mods that add or change map generation may behave unexpectedly.
